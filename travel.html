02

Nearby accommodation

The Montagu Arms in Beaulieu is a nearby hotel option. Beaulieu Inn, Careys Manor and Forest Park Country Hotel give guests further choices around Brockenhurst and the New Forest.

Booking early is sensible for a September weekend in the New Forest.

03

By train

Brockenhurst is the nearest main railway station. The Master Builder’s House is around a 10-minute drive from the station, and Buckler’s Hard advises that taxis are available from Brockenhurst.

Please arrange onward transport in advance rather than relying on finding a taxi at the end of the evening.

04

Taxis

New Forest Taxis – ATS: 07440 402402
Station Taxis Brockenhurst: 01590 545454
Brockenhurst Taxis: 01590 615141

Pre-book both journeys if possible. Buckler’s Hard is lovely; spontaneously sourcing a midnight taxi is rather less lovely.

05

Parking

There is plenty of on-site parking for wedding guests at The Master Builder’s House. The hotel has 70 private parking spaces at the front of the property.

Use SO42 7XB in your sat-nav to be directed to the venue.
